Sample Document - Plain Text Format
====================================

This is a sample document for testing the document processing pipeline.
It contains information about records retention policies.

Section 1: Overview
-------------------
The organization maintains a comprehensive records management program.
All records must be classified according to their retention category.

Section 2: Retention Periods
---------------------------
Financial records: 7 years
Personnel files: 5 years after termination
Contracts: 10 years after expiration
Tax documents: Permanent retention

Section 3: Compliance
--------------------
All departments must comply with the records retention schedule.
Violations may result in regulatory penalties.
